About Evolyra

Evolyra is a menstrual cycle tracking application designed to help users better understand their menstrual cycle, track symptoms, record moods, and recognize personal patterns over time.

The app is designed with privacy in mind. Personal cycle information remains on the user's device and is controlled by the user.

Information Stored in the App

Evolyra allows users to record personal cycle-related information, including:

  • Menstrual cycle dates
  • Cycle history and patterns
  • Symptoms and personal observations
  • Mood tracking
  • Energy levels
  • Sleep information
  • Other self-tracked personal information entered by the user

This information may be considered health-related data because it can provide information about your body and wellbeing.

Information Evolyra Does Not Collect

Evolyra does not collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location data
  • Information from other apps or services

Local Data Storage

All information entered into Evolyra is stored locally on the user's device.

Evolyra does not upload, transmit, or store menstrual health information on external servers.

Your cycle data remains on your device and is not accessible by Evolyra.

If device backup is enabled on your phone, Android may include the app's data in your own backup to your Google account. That backup belongs to you — Evolyra has no access to it. You can turn this off in your device's backup settings.

No Account Required

Evolyra does not require users to create an account.

Your information is not connected to a personal profile, and Evolyra does not maintain a database containing your personal cycle information.

How Your Data Is Used

Your data is only used to provide the features available in the app, including:

  • Showing your cycle history
  • Creating personal estimates
  • Identifying recurring patterns
  • Displaying statistics and insights based on your own logs

Evolyra does not use your information for advertising or for any other purpose.

Data Sharing

Evolyra does not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Your personal health-related information remains private and under your control.

Third-Party Services

Evolyra does not use third-party advertising services, analytics services, or external data collection systems that access your menstrual health information.

Your Control Over Data

You have full control over the information stored in Evolyra.

You can manage your information directly in the app:

  • Export a copy through Settings → Your data → Download my data
  • Delete everything stored locally through Settings → Your data → Clear local data

You can also remove your locally stored data by deleting the application from your device.

Deleted data cannot be restored, because Evolyra has no access to your information.

Security

Your data is protected by the security features of the device where Evolyra is installed.

We recommend using a device passcode or screen lock and keeping your device updated.

Children's Privacy

Evolyra is not designed for children under 13, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

In countries where a higher minimum age applies to the processing of health-related information, the app is intended for users who have reached that age.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect improvements to Evolyra, changes in the application, or updates to applicable privacy requirements.

If Evolyra introduces future features that involve storing data outside your device, such as account or synchronization features, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are activated. Any such features will be clearly explained, and you will have the choice whether to use them.

Any changes will be published on this page.
