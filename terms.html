What Evolyra Is

Evolyra is an application designed to help you track your menstrual cycle and understand your own recurring patterns over time.

The app allows you to record information such as:

  • Cycle dates
  • Mood
  • Energy
  • Sleep
  • Symptoms
  • Other personal observations you choose to record

Evolyra analyzes your own history and provides statistics, patterns, and estimates based on the information you have logged.

The results shown in the app are personal estimates based on previous information and should not be considered facts or guarantees.
