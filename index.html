About Evolyra

Evolyra is a menstrual cycle tracking application designed to help users understand and track their menstrual cycle, symptoms, moods, and personal patterns.

Information Stored in the App

Evolyra allows users to record personal cycle-related information, which may include:

  • Menstrual cycle dates
  • Cycle patterns and history
  • Symptoms and personal notes
  • Mood, energy, sleep, and other self-tracked information

Local Data Storage

All personal information entered into Evolyra is stored locally on your device.

Evolyra does not upload, transmit, or store your menstrual health information on external servers.

No Account Required

Evolyra does not require users to create an account. Your information remains on your own device and is not connected to a personal profile stored by Evolyra.

Data Sharing

Evolyra does not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Your personal health-related information is private and remains under your control.

Third-Party Services

Evolyra does not use third-party analytics, advertising services, or external data collection systems that access your personal cycle information.

Your Control Over Data

You can remove your locally stored Evolyra data at any time by deleting the application from your device or using available device settings.

Children's Privacy

Evolyra is not intended for children under the applicable minimum age required in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect improvements to Evolyra or changes in applicable requirements. Any updates will be published on this page.
