Evolyra Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

About Evolyra

Evolyra is a menstrual cycle tracking application designed to help users understand and track their cycle, symptoms, and personal patterns.

Data Storage

All personal data entered into Evolyra is stored locally on your device.

Evolyra does not collect, sell, share, or transmit your personal information or menstrual health data to external servers.

Accounts

Evolyra does not require user accounts and does not store personal information outside your device.

Your Data

You remain in control of your information. Removing the application from your device will remove locally stored application data.
